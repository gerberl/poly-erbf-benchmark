Several patterns emerge.
\mn{erbf} selects Lipschitz-guided centre placement in 53.5\% of folds and local ridge width initialisation in 63.3\%.
Fixed centre counts are selected in 70.9\% of folds, with the \texttt{auto} rule accounting for the remaining 29.1\%; the median effective centre count is 62.8 after averaging within each dataset.
\mn{chebypoly} selects relatively high polynomial degrees (median~10, IQR 4.2--12.5), with interactions enabled in 77.8\% of folds.
\mn{chebytree} uses a median depth of 5 (7.2 leaves) and lower-degree leaf polynomials (median~2.8).
\mn{xgb} selects high learning rates (median~0.292); its median best-iteration index is 30 (IQR 17.9--59.1), well below the 2\,000-round limit.
\mn{rf} selects a median of 350.2 trees, with a feature fraction of 0.7 chosen in 69.8\% of folds.
